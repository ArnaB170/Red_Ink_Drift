SYLLABUS & GRADING RULES

⭕

1. Circle Terrible Answers to Grade Them

Your pen never stops moving and leaves a lethal red ink trail. Steer around an ungraded student answer (like "2 + 2 = 5") and close the loop!

💡 TIP: Closing a loop around an answer is SAFE—it dissolves that ink loop, stamps a giant red grade, and builds your Combo Multiplier!

💀

2. Avoid Self-Collisions & Giant Erasers

Crossing your own ink trail without enclosing a student answer causes an immediate ink-splatter crash! Also watch out for patrolling Pink Pearl Erasers—they telegraph their turns with dashed arrows.

🤍

3. White-Out Puddles & 60s Coffee Spill Boss

Passing through White-Out Correction Fluid coats your nib and locks your steering straight ahead for 1.0 second. Every 60 seconds, a Coffee Mug tips over—steer into the safe zone before the espresso wave floods the sheet!

TOUCH / MOUSE: Touch & drag anywhere on the paper to steer
KEYBOARD: A / D or Left / Right Arrows
BOOST: Spacebar / Shift / Boost Button
